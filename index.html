Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Do parents need to visit campus to pay fees?

Arambh X is designed to support digital fee payments, so parents can pay without making a trip to campus just for the payment.

How does Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform support the fee journey?

Arambh X is designed to automate fee reminders, payment-link messages and parent updates through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Will parents receive a payment receipt?

Electronic receipts are part of the intended fee-payment journey, giving families a digital record after payment.

Who is Arambh X for?

Arambh X is designed for both schools and colleges, and the families who pay their fees. The platform is managed by Infotech Business Solutions.
